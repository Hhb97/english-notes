# 英文自我介绍与项目经历

> [!ABSTRACT]
> - "Tell me about yourself" 的公式：**现在（Present）— 过去（Past）— 未来（Future）**，控制在六十秒左右。
> - 讲项目用四步：**背景 → 你的角色 → 技术栈 → 结果（带数字）**。
> - 面试官想听的是「你做成了什么」，所以多用 **I led / I designed / I reduced**，少用 **we did**。
> - 谈离职原因永远往前看：讲你想要什么，不讲前公司哪里不好。

## 一、六十秒自我介绍：Present – Past – Future

面试官说 "Tell me about yourself"，不是想听你的出生地和大学专业，而是想快速判断：你现在做什么、过去积累了什么、为什么适合这个岗位。

三段式结构：

1. **Present**：现在的职位、公司类型、主要职责。
2. **Past**：之前的经历里最相关的一两件事，带一个成果。
3. **Future**：你想做什么，为什么这个岗位正好合适。

### 第一段：现在

```phrases
Thanks for having me. I'm a backend engineer with six years of experience. | /θæŋks fɔːr ˈhævɪŋ miː aɪm ə ˈbækend ˈendʒəˈnɪr wɪð sɪks jɪrz ʌv ɪkˈspɪriəns/ | 谢谢邀请我。我是一名有六年经验的后端工程师。 | 开场先道谢，再直接说职位和年限
Right now I'm a senior engineer on the payments team at a mid-size fintech company. | /raɪt naʊ aɪm ə ˈsiːnjər ˈendʒəˈnɪr ɑːn ðə ˈpeɪmənts tiːm æt ə mɪd saɪz ˈfɪnˌtek ˈkʌmpəˌniː/ | 目前我在一家中型金融科技公司的支付团队做高级工程师。
My day-to-day work is building and maintaining the services behind checkout. | /maɪ deɪ tuː deɪ wɜːrk ɪz ˈbɪldɪŋ ənd meɪnˈteɪnɪŋ ðə ˈsɜːrvəsəz bɪˈhaɪnd ˈtʃeˌkaʊt/ | 我日常工作是搭建和维护结账背后的服务。
I mostly work in Java and Go, and I also do a fair amount of infrastructure work. | /aɪ ˈmoʊstli wɜːrk ɪn ˈdʒɑːvə ənd ɡoʊ ənd aɪ ˈɔːlsoʊ duː ə fer əˈmaʊnt ʌv ˌɪnfrəˈstrʌktʃər wɜːrk/ | 我主要用 Java 和 Go，也做不少基础设施方面的工作。 | a fair amount of 表示「相当多」，比 a lot of 更克制
I'm responsible for the reliability of three core services. | /aɪm riˈspɑːnsəbəl fɔːr ðə riˌlaɪəˈbɪləti ʌv θriː kɔːr ˈsɜːrvəsəz/ | 我负责三个核心服务的稳定性。
```

### 第二段：过去

```phrases

One project I'm proud of is a rewrite of our order service. | /wʌn ˈprɑːdʒekt aɪm praʊd ʌv ɪz ə riˈraɪt ʌv ˈaʊər ˈɔːrdər ˈsɜːrvəs/ | 我比较自豪的一个项目是重写订单服务。
```

### 第三段：未来

```phrases
Now I'm looking for a role where I can work on larger-scale systems. | /naʊ aɪm ˈlʊkɪŋ fɔːr ə roʊl wer aɪ kæn wɜːrk ɑːn ˈlɑːrdʒər skeɪl ˈsɪstəmz/ | 现在我想找一个能接触更大规模系统的岗位。

I'd love to bring my experience with high-traffic services to your team. | /aɪd lʌv tuː brɪŋ maɪ ɪkˈspɪriəns wɪð haɪ ˈtræfɪk ˈsɜːrvəsəz tuː jɔːr tiːm/ | 我很想把我在高流量服务上的经验带到你们团队。
I've followed your engineering blog for a while, and the work on observability really stood out. | /aɪv ˈfɑːloʊd jɔːr ˈendʒəˈnɪrɪŋ blɔːɡ fɔːr ə waɪl ənd ðə wɜːrk ɑːn əbˌzɜːrvəˈbɪləti ˈrɪli stʊd aʊt/ | 我关注你们的技术博客有一段时间了，可观测性方面的工作特别吸引我。 | 提具体的东西，比 I like your company 有说服力
That's why I applied, and I'm happy to go deeper on any of this. | /ðæts waɪ aɪ əˈplaɪd ənd aɪm ˈhæpi tuː ɡoʊ ˈdiːpər ɑːn ˈeni ʌv ðɪs/ | 这就是我投递的原因，上面任何一点我都很乐意展开讲。 | 收尾把话头交还给面试官
```

### 示范稿一：有经验的后端工程师

```text
Thanks for having me. I'm a backend engineer with six years of experience.
Right now I'm a senior engineer on the payments team at a mid-size fintech
company. I mostly work in Java and Go, and I'm responsible for three core
services that handle checkout.

Before that, I spent three years at a startup, where I was one of the first
engineers. One project I'm proud of is a rewrite of our order service. I led
the migration, and it cut our error rate by sixty percent.

Now I'm looking for a role where I can work on larger-scale systems. What
excites me about this position is the focus on data infrastructure, and I'd
love to bring my experience with high-traffic services to your team.
```

### 示范稿二：转岗或应届、经验不多的人

经验少不要硬撑年限，把重点放在项目、学习速度和想做的方向上。

```text
Hi, I'm Lin. I recently finished my master's degree in computer science,
and I've been working as a junior test engineer for the past year and a half.

In my current role, I built an automated regression suite for our mobile
app. It brought our release testing time down from two days to four hours.
Along the way, I picked up Python, CI pipelines, and a lot about how to
write stable tests.

Next, I'd like to move toward a developer role where I can write production
code and keep testing at the center of how I build. This team's focus on
quality is a big reason I applied.
```

```phrases
I recently finished my master's degree in computer science. | /aɪ ˈriːsəntli ˈfɪnɪʃt maɪ ˈmæstərz dɪˈɡriː ɪn kəmˈpjuːtər ˈsaɪəns/ | 我最近完成了计算机硕士学位。
I've been working as a junior test engineer for the past year and a half. | /aɪv bɪn ˈwɜːrkɪŋ æz ə ˈdʒuːnjər test ˈendʒəˈnɪr fɔːr ðə pæst jɪr ənd ə hæf/ | 过去一年半我一直做初级测试工程师。
I built an automated regression suite for our mobile app. | /aɪ bɪlt æn ˈɔːtəˌmeɪtɪd rəˈɡreʃən swiːt fɔːr ˈaʊər ˈmoʊbəl æp/ | 我为我们的移动应用搭建了自动化回归测试集。
It brought our release testing time down from two days to four hours. | /ɪt brɔːt ˈaʊər riˈliːs ˈtestɪŋ taɪm daʊn frʌm tuː deɪz tuː fɔːr ˈaʊərz/ | 它把发布测试时间从两天缩短到四个小时。
Along the way, I picked up Python and CI pipelines. | /əˈlɔːŋ ðə weɪ aɪ pɪkt ʌp ˈpaɪθɑːn ənd siː aɪ ˈpaɪˌplaɪnz/ | 在这个过程中，我学会了 Python 和 CI 流水线。 | pick up 表示「顺带学会」，很自然
I'd like to move toward a developer role where testing stays at the center. | /aɪd laɪk tuː muːv təˈwɔːrd ə dɪˈveləpər roʊl wer ˈtestɪŋ steɪz æt ðə ˈsentər/ | 我想转向开发岗位，同时让测试一直是我做事的核心。
```

> [!TIP] 六十秒大约一百五十个词
> 稿子太长会让面试官失去耐心，太短又显得没准备。写完大声读一遍并计时，控制在一分钟左右。不要背成播音腔，记住三段的骨架和关键数字就够了。

## 二、怎么讲一个项目：背景、角色、技术、结果

面试官让你 "Walk me through a project" 时，按固定顺序讲，听的人不会迷路。

### 1. 背景：为什么要做

```phrases
The project was about moving our monolith to microservices. | /ðə ˈprɑːdʒekt wɑːz əˈbaʊt ˈmuːvɪŋ ˈaʊər ˈmɑːnəˌlɪθ tuː ˈmaɪkroʊˌsɜːrvɪsɪz/ | 这个项目是把我们的单体应用拆成微服务。
We had a problem: deployments took two hours and often failed. | /wiː hæd ə ˈprɑːbləm dɪˈplɔɪmənts tʊk tuː ˈaʊərz ənd ˈɔːfən feɪld/ | 我们遇到了问题：一次部署要两个小时，而且经常失败。
The goal was to make releases safer and faster. | /ðə ɡoʊl wɑːz tuː meɪk rɪˈliːsɪz ˈseɪfər ənd ˈfæstər/ | 目标是让发布更安全、更快。
The business needed it because we were launching in two new markets. | /ðə ˈbɪznəs ˈniːdəd ɪt bɪˈkɔːz wiː wɜːr ˈlɔːntʃɪŋ ɪn tuː nuː ˈmɑːrkəts/ | 业务上需要它，因为我们要在两个新市场上线。
```

### 2. 你的角色：你具体做了什么

```phrases
I was the tech lead for a team of five. | /aɪ wɑːz ðə tek led fɔːr ə tiːm ʌv faɪv/ | 我是一个五人团队的技术负责人。
I owned the design and the rollout of the new payment flow. | /aɪ oʊnd ðə dɪˈzaɪn ənd ðə ˈroʊˌlaʊt ʌv ðə nuː ˈpeɪmənt floʊ/ | 我负责新支付流程的设计和上线。
My part was the data model and the API layer. | /maɪ pɑːrt wɑːz ðə ˈdeɪtə ˈmɑːdəl ənd ðə ˌeɪˌpiːˈaɪ ˈleɪər/ | 我负责的部分是数据模型和接口层。
I worked closely with product and the QA team. | /aɪ wɜːrkt ˈkloʊsli wɪð ˈprɑːdəkt ənd ðə kjuː eɪ tiːm/ | 我和产品及测试团队密切合作。
I wasn't the only one involved, but I drove the technical decisions. | /aɪ ˈwɑːzənt ðə ˈoʊnli wʌn ˌɪnˈvɑːlvd bʌt aɪ droʊv ðə ˈteknɪkəl dɪˈsɪʒənz/ | 参与的不止我一个人，但技术决策是我主导的。 | 诚实又不埋没自己的贡献
```

> [!WARNING] we 和 I 要分清
> 中文习惯说「我们做了……」，直译成 **We built…** 会让面试官分不清你到底做了什么。团队成果可以先说 we，再马上补一句 **My part was…** 或 **I specifically…**。

### 3. 技术栈：用了什么，为什么选

```phrases
The stack was Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, Kafka, and Kubernetes. | /ðə stæk wɑːz sprɪŋ buːt ˈpoʊstɡres ˌkjuː ˈel ˈkɑːfkə ənd ˌkuːbərˈnetiːz/ | 技术栈是 Spring Boot、PostgreSQL、Kafka 和 Kubernetes。
We chose Kafka because we needed reliable, asynchronous messaging. | /wiː tʃoʊz ˈkɑːfkə bɪˈkɔːz wiː ˈniːdəd rɪˈlaɪəbəl ˈeɪˈsɪŋkrənəs ˈmesɪdʒɪŋ/ | 我们选 Kafka 是因为需要可靠的异步消息。
I picked Postgres over MongoDB because our data was highly relational. | /aɪ pɪkt ˈpoʊstɡres ˈoʊvər ˈmɑːŋɡoʊ ˌdiː ˈbiː bɪˈkɔːz ˈaʊər ˈdeɪtə wɑːz ˈhaɪli riˈleɪʃənəl/ | 我选 Postgres 而不是 MongoDB，因为我们的数据关系性很强。 | A over B 是讲取舍的常用结构
We deployed everything with a CI/CD pipeline on GitHub Actions. | /wiː dɪˈplɔɪd ˈevriˌθɪŋ wɪð ə siː aɪ ˌsiːˈdiː ˈpaɪˌplaɪn ɑːn ˈɡɪthʌb ˈækʃənz/ | 我们用 GitHub Actions 的 CI/CD 流水线部署一切。
I'm most comfortable with Java, but I've used Python for tooling. | /aɪm moʊst ˈkʌmfərtəbəl wɪð ˈdʒɑːvə bʌt aɪv juːzd ˈpaɪθɑːn fɔːr ˈtuːlɪŋ/ | 我最熟练的是 Java，不过也用 Python 写过工具。
```

### 4. 结果：用数字说话

```phrases
As a result, deployment time dropped from two hours to ten minutes. | /æz ə rɪˈzʌlt dɪˈplɔɪmənt taɪm drɑːpt frʌm tuː ˈaʊərz tuː ten ˈmɪnəts/ | 结果，部署时间从两个小时降到了十分钟。
The change reduced our incident count by about forty percent. | /ðə tʃeɪndʒ rəˈduːst ˈaʊər ˈɪnsədənt kaʊnt baɪ əˈbaʊt ˈfɔːrti pərˈsent/ | 这个改动让我们的故障数量减少了大约百分之四十。
We improved the response time of the checkout API by three hundred milliseconds. | /wiː ˌɪmˈpruːvd ðə rɪˈspɑːns taɪm ʌv ðə ˈtʃeˌkaʊt ˌeɪˌpiːˈaɪ baɪ θriː ˈhʌndrəd ˈmɪlɪˌsekəndz/ | 我们把结账接口的响应时间缩短了三百毫秒。
It saved the team roughly ten hours of manual work every week. | /ɪt seɪvd ðə tiːm ˈrʌfli ten ˈaʊərz ʌv ˈmænjuəl wɜːrk ˈevəri wiːk/ | 它每周为团队节省了大约十个小时的手工工作。
It went live on time, and we had zero downtime during the cutover. | /ɪt went laɪv ɑːn taɪm ənd wiː hæd ˈzɪroʊ ˈdaʊntaɪm ˈdʊrɪŋ ðə ˈkʌtˌoʊvər/ | 它按时上线，切换期间零停机。
```

### 示范稿三：讲一个项目（约一分半）

```text
One project I'd like to highlight is the migration of our checkout service.

The problem was that our monolith was slow to deploy. A release took about
two hours and failed one time out of five. We were launching in two new
markets, so we needed to ship faster.

I was the tech lead for a team of five. I designed the new service
boundaries and owned the data migration. We used Spring Boot, PostgreSQL,
and Kafka, and we deployed on Kubernetes. I chose Kafka because we needed
reliable asynchronous messaging between services.

As a result, deployment time dropped from two hours to ten minutes, and
incidents went down by about forty percent. The biggest lesson was to
migrate in small steps, with a rollback plan at every stage.
```

```phrases
A release took about two hours and failed one time out of five. | /ə riˈliːs tʊk əˈbaʊt tuː ˈaʊərz ənd feɪld wʌn taɪm aʊt ʌv faɪv/ | 一次发布大约要两小时，而且五次里有一次失败。

```

## 三、说清楚你的优势

美式面试鼓励你明确说出自己的优势，但要配一个具体例子，不要只贴形容词。

```phrases
I'd say my biggest strength is breaking down complex problems. | /aɪd seɪ maɪ ˈbɪɡəst streŋkθ ɪz ˈbreɪkɪŋ daʊn ˈkɑːmpleks ˈprɑːbləmz/ | 我觉得我最大的优势是拆解复杂问题。
```

## 四、为什么选这家公司，为什么离开现在的公司

> [!CAUTION] 千万别说前公司或老板的坏话
> 中文里说 **公司没前途、领导不行、加班太多** 很自然，但在英文面试里这些直译会让人觉得你难共事。
> - 不要说：**My boss is terrible and the company has no future.**
> - 改成：**I've learned a lot there, and now I'm looking for new challenges and more room to grow.**
> - 加班问题可以说：**I'm looking for a team with a more sustainable pace.**

